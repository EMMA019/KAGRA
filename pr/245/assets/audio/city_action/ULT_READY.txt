"Ult Ready" — the city_action ult-ready chime.

An original sound effect composed and synthesized for KAGRA: a
two-bell ping — an E6 bell (inharmonic partials with exponential
decays) answered 70 ms later by a quieter B6 bell — struck once at
the moment the ultimate's gauge tops out and its button lights.
0.6 s. Fully synthesized from oscillators and a breath of filtered
noise; no third-party samples, recordings or material.
