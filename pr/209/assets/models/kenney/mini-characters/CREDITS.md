Used by Moonlit Swarm (survivor arena) as the five hero skins. Each file is a copy made with
`tools/gen_assets/pack_glb.py --clip holding-both-shoot --time 0.1` from `Models/GLB format/character-<id>.glb`:
the arms-forward frame of the pack's own shooting clip is baked into the vertices (so the hero holds a gun
forward, not in a T-pose), animations, skins and joint weights dropped, the colormap texture embedded.

- character-female-a.glb, character-male-c.glb, character-male-a.glb, character-female-d.glb, character-male-e.glb
