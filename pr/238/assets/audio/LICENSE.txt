KAGRA synthesized audio
=======================

The WAV files in this directory (`jump.wav`, `fire.wav`, `card.wav`, `bgm.wav`)
are original tones synthesized for KAGRA. They are not recordings of anyone
else's work.

They are dedicated to the public domain under the CC0 1.0 Universal dedication.

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

You can copy, modify, and ship them with the Windows zip and the Android APK.
No attribution is required. The engine still plays if a device is missing:
the mixer no-ops instead of failing the frame.
