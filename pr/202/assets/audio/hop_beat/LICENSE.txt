HOP-BEAT music
==============

hop_beat_loop.(mp3|ogg), hop_beat_gameover.*, hop_beat_newbest.*, hop_beat_revive.*
and hop_beat_beatgrid.json are original works, synthesised by the KAGRA procedural
music kit (kagra_music) from oscillator and noise maths. They contain no samples,
no recordings, no AI-model output and no third-party material. The composition
(chords, melodies, rhythms) is original.

They are dedicated to the public domain under the CC0 1.0 Universal dedication.

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No attribution is required. Provenance and rebuild parameters: docs/provenance/hop_beat_music.json in the KAGRA repository


HOP-BEAT effects
================

hop.wav, land.wav, perfect.wav, good.wav, combo.wav, hit.wav, fall.wav and stomp.wav
are original works written by tools/gen_assets/hop_audio.py from oscillator and noise
maths in plain Python (no samples, no recordings, no third-party material). They are
dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 as well; `python3 tools/gen_assets/hop_audio.py --check`
rebuilds them byte for byte.
