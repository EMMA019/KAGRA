Neon Cat placeholder sounds
===========================

The WAV files in this directory are original tones synthesized by
`tools/gen_assets/cat_audio.py` from sine, square and noise maths. They are
not recordings of anyone else's work and use no samples.

They are dedicated to the public domain under the CC0 1.0 Universal dedication.

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No attribution is required. They are placeholders: swap any file for your own
(free assets or SUNO) and point `audio` in `games/neon_cat_minimal.json` at it.
