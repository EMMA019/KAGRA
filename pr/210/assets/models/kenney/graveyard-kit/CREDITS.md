Used by Moonlit Swarm (survivor arena): the ghost, zombie, skeleton, vampire and keeper foes and the arena dressing. Each file is a
rest-pose copy made with `tools/gen_assets/pack_glb.py` from `Models/GLB format/<id>.glb`: animations
dropped, the colormap texture embedded. Nothing else changed.

- character-ghost.glb, character-zombie.glb, character-skeleton.glb, character-vampire.glb, character-keeper.glb
- gravestone-cross.glb, gravestone-round.glb, gravestone-broken.glb, cross-wood.glb, rocks.glb
- pine.glb, pine-crooked.glb, pumpkin.glb, pumpkin-carved.glb, lantern-candle.glb
