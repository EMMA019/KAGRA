"Harlem Night" — the city_action BGM.

An original loop composed and synthesized for KAGRA: a hard-bop /
funk groove in the shape of the game's Harlem-night stage — a 12-bar
Bb blues chorus at 126 BPM on a swung eighth grid, with a walking
bass, ride cymbal, feathered kick, brushy snare, electric-piano
comping and brass-section stabs. Fully synthesized from oscillators
and filtered noise; no third-party samples, recordings or material.
