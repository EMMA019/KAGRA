"Boss Stomp" — the city_action boss-fight BGM (loop).

An original loop composed and synthesized for KAGRA: a driving
minor-funk groove for the boss fights — an 8-bar Bb-minor form at
140 BPM on a straight 16th grid, with a syncopated picked-bass riff,
four-on-the-floor kick with syncopated pushes, 16th-note hats, a
hard backbeat snare, dark organ stabs, and a minor-2nd alarm figure
in the turnaround bar. Fully synthesized from oscillators and
filtered noise; no third-party samples, recordings or material.
