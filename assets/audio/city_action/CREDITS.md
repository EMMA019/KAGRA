28.0 s seamless stereo loop at 112 kbit/s MP3: filtered-noise wind
with slow periodic gusts and a faint band-passed whistle over a low
sine-cluster drone (54 / 81.2 / 108.6 Hz), made for the ?look=bg
eerie-streets mode — it layers quietly under the stage BGM, cued and
re-cued by the kit as a long one-shot. Fully synthesized from
oscillators and filtered noise (numpy); no third-party samples,
recordings or material. Loop check (2026-11-18): every modulation
period divides the 28 s loop and the noise layers close the seam
with a 1.5 s equal-power crossfade of tail into head, so the wrap is
seamless; not auditioned by ear.
